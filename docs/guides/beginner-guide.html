Honor of Kings: ACE — Beginner Guide

01Basic rules

Learn the essentials and get ready for your first match.

01

Choose your tactician

Six tacticians compete in the ACE Tournament. Each starts with 60 Health.

02

Build your lineup

Buy cards to build a lineup. Each round, you face a randomly selected opponent. The winner deals damage to the loser’s Health.

03

Find synergies

Use your heroes’ card effects together to strengthen your lineup.

04

Be the last survivor

A tactician is eliminated at 0 Health. The last tactician standing wins.

Secret Techniques

04

Level up

Spend Energy to raise your tactician’s level. Each level-up unlocks one free talent. Levels marked with a unit-count icon also grant +1 deployment slot. Higher tactician levels increase the chance of finding higher-tier hero cards.

05

Free tactician rotation

Before Platinum, free tacticians unlock according to your rank. After you reach Platinum, the standard weekly free rotation begins the following week.

01

Choose one of three

Each tactician level-up lets you choose one of three talents for free. Talents come in tier 1 (Bronze), tier 2 (Silver), and tier 3 (Gold); higher tiers are stronger. All six players receive the same talent tier at the same level in a match. At Silver rank and above, you can refresh your talent choices once per match.

01

Earning Energy

You receive a fixed amount of Energy each round. Unspent Energy carries over. Selling a hero card gives 1 Energy; selling a deployed hero also gives 1 Energy. Some talents and effect cards grant Energy too.

02

Spending Energy

Hero cards always cost 3 Energy. An effect card’s cost appears in its upper-right corner. Leveling your tactician also costs Energy; the level-up button shows the current cost.

01

Place a bid

At set rounds, everyone enters an auction for hero cards, effect cards, and other rewards. Select an item and press Bid. There is no bidding cap. The highest bidder wins, and unsuccessful bids cost no Energy. If bids are tied, the earlier bid wins.

02

Auction dividends

Effective bids go into a dividend pool. When the auction ends, the pool’s Energy is distributed among all players. Players with lower Health receive a larger share.

03

Collect waiting-area drops

While players wait, random items drop every few seconds. More waiting players means more drops. Interaction items are most common; Energy and hero cards are rarer. Energy drops give 1 Energy, and hero drops are tier 1 or tier 2.

Three Kingdoms · Deploy

Main carry: Lu Bu

Core lineup

Lu Bu, Cai Yan, Zhao Yun, Diaochan, Xiao Qiao, Zhou Yu, Cao Cao

How it works

Cao Cao’s Prepare effect triggers Three Kingdoms heroes’ Deploy effects, steadily raising the team’s levels. Cai Yan adds levels when Three Kingdoms hero cards are played; Xiao Qiao adds more levels when allies trigger Deploy. Build a strong Lu Bu as your main carry.

Practical tips

  • Until you find Zhao Yun, Diaochan, and Zhou Yu, use other Three Kingdoms or Deploy heroes, such as Lady Zhen and Arke.
  • In the final rounds, use Last Will or Change of Commander to transfer another hero’s high level to Lu Bu.
  • Give Zhou Yu defensive equipment and a Mana Chalice so he survives and casts sooner.

Round-by-round plan

Rounds 1–3
Prioritize Sun Shangxiang; use Yi Xing or Niu Mo if needed. Reach tactician level 2 in round 2, then look for Cai Yan, Cao Cao, and Zhao Yun.
Rounds 4–7
Complete Cai Yan, Cao Cao, Zhao Yun, and Diaochan. Reach level 3 after finding the core cards. Prioritize awakening Cao Cao and Cai Yan. If Change of Commander appears, buy two Sun Shangxiang cards. Reach level 4 and add Xiao Qiao.
Rounds 8–11
Reach level 5 quickly and find Lu Bu and Zhou Yu. Deploy Lu Bu, transfer Sun Shangxiang’s levels with Change of Commander, then sell her to deploy Zhou Yu.
Round 12+
If the final battle looks uncertain, replace Zhao Yun with Taiyi Zhenren to revive Lu Bu.

Recommended equipment

Lu Bu
Blazing Embrace, Infinity Edge, Red Demon
Cao Cao
Guardian’s Glory, Spirit Ward, Eye of the Phoenix

Recommended tacticians

Bai Ge
Copies after every four combinations help awaken heroes. His Secret Technique can duplicate the cards you need.
Marco
Playing four tier 3 or lower hero cards earns treasure cards for resources or combat power. This suits the lineup’s many low-tier heroes.
Chang Xiao'e
Her skill grants levels steadily. Wait until four heroes are deployed before using her Secret Technique.

Recommended talents

Generosity
When your heroes trigger Deploy, two random Three Kingdoms heroes gain 2 levels.
Take Command
Receive a card that sets a Three Kingdoms hero’s level equal to the nearest other Three Kingdoms hero’s level.

Hero card effects

Lu Bu
Engage: The three highest-level Three Kingdoms heroes gain 20% physical and magical lifesteal. Double this effect for Lu Bu.
Zhao Yun
Deploy: This hero and two random other heroes gain 1 level.
Cao Cao
Prepare: Trigger one random Three Kingdoms hero’s Deploy effect.
Cai Yan
Whenever you play a Three Kingdoms hero card, the three lowest-level Three Kingdoms heroes gain 1 level.
Diaochan
Deploy: All Three Kingdoms heroes gain 1 level.
Xiao Qiao
Whenever an allied hero triggers Deploy, three random allied heroes gain 1 level.
Zhou Yu
Deploy: All allied heroes gain 1 level.

Sunset Sea · Prepare

Main carry: Angela

Core lineup

Angela, Dolia, Biron, Allain, Milady, Athena, Heino

How it works

Use the Prepare effects of Biron, Allain, Milady, and Angela with Dolia to grow your heroes and the Arcomia Core. Add Athena and Heino later to accelerate that growth. At combat start, the Core distributes its levels as Temporary Levels among Sunset Sea heroes; a single hero can receive at most 50% of the total. Athena’s Engage effect triggers allied Prepare effects.

Practical tips

  • Buy Ocean Treasure when it appears for faction equipment that improves damage and durability.
  • Against Flash heroes, surround Angela with allies to protect her.
  • For the deciding battle, consider keeping only Athena and one carry—Angela or Heino—among your Sunset Sea heroes, concentrating the Core’s levels on the carry.

Round-by-round plan

Rounds 1–3
Prioritize Biron to grow the Core and strengthen your early board. Reach level 2 in round 2.
Rounds 4–7
Reach level 3 and buy Milady and Allain. Find Dolia and Angela, and awaken them to increase the team’s growth.
Rounds 8–11
Reach level 5 and deploy Athena and Heino. Continue awakening heroes to strengthen their keyword effects and level the Core faster.
Round 12+
If you increase your deployment limit, add Ying to trigger Prepare and accelerate team and Core growth.

Recommended equipment

Angela
Mana Chalice, Void Staff, Blazing Embrace
Athena
Overlord’s Armor, Guardian’s Glory, Eye of the Phoenix

Recommended tacticians

Xiangxiang
Her attacks scale with your tier 1–3 heroes, making her a good fit for this low-tier lineup.
Chang Xiao'e
Reliable level growth each round. Use her Secret Technique after deploying at least four heroes.
Naonao
If Sunset Sea is offered by her skill, choose it and use the skill each round to find your faction heroes faster.

Recommended talents

Energy Siphon
When you sell a deployed hero, the Arcomia Core gains that hero’s levels.
Smart Armor
Receive random Sunset Sea equipment. An allied hero wearing it triggers Prepare one additional time.

Hero card effects

Angela
Prepare: This hero and the Arcomia Core gain 2 levels.
Biron
Prepare: The Arcomia Core gains 2 levels.
Allain
Prepare: One Sunset Sea hero with the most equipment gains 2 levels. The Arcomia Core gains 1 level.
Milady
Summons gain 3 temporary levels. Prepare: Double this temporary level bonus; the Arcomia Core gains 3 additional levels.
Dolia
Whenever an allied hero triggers Prepare, three random allied heroes and the Arcomia Core gain 1 level.
Athena
Engage: Trigger two random Sunset Sea heroes’ Prepare effects.
Heino
Whenever the Arcomia Core gains levels, this hero gains 1 level.

Core lineup

Ao Yin, Consort Yu, Liang, Shao Siyuan, Guiguzi, Augran, Donghuang

How it works

Guiguzi, Shao Siyuan, and Augran summon Seer, Destiny, and Afterlife Totems. Their levels grow with their summoners. More Totems give Ao Yin more Attack Speed and Temporary Levels during combat. Consort Yu and Liang add team levels. Donghuang protects the Totems and gains Temporary Levels from them. Seer provides healing, Destiny grants levels, and Afterlife helps the frontline absorb damage.

Practical tips

  • Ao Yin is the late-game carry. Surround him with heroes and Totems to protect him from backline attacks.
  • In the final rounds, replace Liang with Taiyi Zhenren to protect Ao Yin, or use Yaria with control equipment for extra safety.

Round-by-round plan

Rounds 1–3
Use Niu Mo and Gan & Mo early. Reach level 2 in round 3. These heroes are temporary and can be replaced when space is tight.
Rounds 4–7
Reach level 3 quickly. Use free refreshes to find Consort Yu, Guiguzi, Liang, and Shao Siyuan. Give damage gear to Consort Yu. Once you have each of these heroes, reach level 4 and find Augran.
Rounds 8–11
Reach level 5 and find Ao Yin, Donghuang, and Augran. Transfer Consort Yu’s damage equipment to Ao Yin.
Round 12+
Keep awakening your core heroes. Add Taiyi Zhenren if you have space, or another utility hero such as Yaria to suit the opponent.

Recommended equipment

Ao Yin
Frostscar, Daybreaker, Nature Staff
Donghuang
Overlord’s Armor, Guardian’s Glory, Eye of the Phoenix

Recommended tacticians

Xiangxiang
Her damage scales with the number and level of your tier 1–3 heroes.
Yaria
Her fawn shares frontline damage with Donghuang and prolongs battles, protecting a carry who grows stronger during combat.
Chang Xiao'e
Her skill expands your level advantage, and her exclusive talent adds team stat bonuses.

Recommended talents

Copier
Copy deployed heroes to awaken core cards faster.
Riverlands Reinforcements
Gain Riverlands heroes to complete the lineup sooner.

Hero card effects

Ao Yin
In combat, each Totem grants Ao Yin 16% Attack Speed and 4 Temporary Levels every 4 seconds.
Consort Yu
Engage: For each Totem on the field, this hero and two random other Riverlands heroes gain 1 level.
Guiguzi
While deployed, summon a Seer Totem.
Shao Siyuan
While deployed, summon a Destiny Totem.
Liang
Whenever an allied unit triggers Engage, the three highest-level allies on the field permanently gain 1 level.
Augran
While deployed, summon an Afterlife Totem. Whenever you trigger Prepare, the Afterlife Totem permanently gains 50% of its summoner’s Attack.
Donghuang
Totems cannot be attacked first. Engage: Gain Temporary Levels equal to 15% of each Totem’s level.

Core lineup

Baili Xuance, Shangguan Wan’er, Dun Shan, Cheng Yaojin, Zhong Kui, Taiyi Zhenren, Li Yuanfang

How it works

Li Yuanfang, Dun Shan, Cheng Yaojin, and Shangguan Wan’er generate Ancient Coins. Spend them on Baili Xuance to keep raising his level. Xuance also gains levels from the previous round’s kills. Zhong Kui lowers enemy levels, while Taiyi Zhenren provides a revive and gives Xuance more time to deal damage.

Practical tips

  • Prioritize equipment on Xuance to support both damage and survival. Spreading resources too thin weakens your carry.
  • Place Li Yuanfang and Taiyi Zhenren next to Xuance so their coins and revive reach him.
  • In the deciding rounds, adjust Xuance’s Flash position to target the enemy carry directly.

Round-by-round plan

Rounds 1–3
Buy Li Yuanfang or Yi Xing for early strength. Reach level 2 in round 3 and find Xuance. Pick up Dun Shan and use his Ancient Coins on Xuance.
Rounds 4–7
Reach level 3, refresh the shop, and try to awaken Xuance. Find Cheng Yaojin and Zhong Kui for frontline strength and control.
Rounds 8–11
Reach level 4 and find Shangguan Wan’er and Taiyi Zhenren. Reach level 5 before round 11. Awaken deployed heroes to improve the board.
Round 12+
Keep finding equipment for Xuance and awakening your heroes. If Xuance is not strong enough, consider Last Will or Transmute to move levels to Li Xin or Mulan.

Naonao

Core lineup

Meng Ya, Mi Yue, Xi Shi, Bai Qi, Old Master, Zhuang Zhou, Luban No. 7

How it works

Xi Shi, Bai Qi, Old Master, Zhuang Zhou, and Luban No. 7 generate Tactics Cards. Playing effect cards triggers Mi Yue, who grants levels to your Conquest hero with the most equipment. Keep that hero as Meng Ya and keep playing cards to grow your main carry.

Practical tips

  • Mi Yue targets the Conquest hero with the most equipment. Ensure Meng Ya has more equipment than everyone else so another hero does not receive the levels.
  • If there is no spare deployment slot in the final rounds, sell Old Master and add Taiyi Zhenren to protect Meng Ya.
  • Give Meng Ya priority on effect cards, equipment, and other resources to build combat power quickly.

Round-by-round plan

Rounds 1–3
Use Yi Xing early. Reach level 2 in round 2 and prioritize finding Mi Yue.
Rounds 4–7
Reach level 3 and collect Mi Yue, Xi Shi, Old Master, and Bai Qi. Awaken Mi Yue, then reach level 4. If you cannot awaken her, avoid staying at a low level so long that you fall behind.
Rounds 8–11
Reach level 5 quickly and find Meng Ya, Zhuang Zhou, and Luban No. 7. Awaken Mi Yue, Xi Shi, and Bai Qi first to produce more Tactics Cards, then the other three.
Round 12+
Continue awakening heroes and searching for Meng Ya and Luban No. 7. With spare slots, add Taiyi Zhenren or Hai Yue for utility and safety.

Recommended equipment

Meng Ya
Infinity Edge, Blazing Embrace, Mana Chalice
Bai Qi
Overlord’s Armor, Eye of the Phoenix, Ominous Premonition

Recommended tacticians

Bai Ge
Old Master and Luban No. 7 generate Tactics Cards through combinations. Bai Ge provides hero copies for more combinations and more Tactics Cards.
Little Daji
Provides more effect cards and additional triggers, helping Mi Yue raise hero levels.
Naonao
Choose Conquest if offered to find heroes without spending so much Energy refreshing. At maximum level, choose a faction talent to strengthen the board.

Recommended talents

Spring Rain
Receive a copy of a deployed Conquest hero; receive two if you won the battle.
Swift Orders
Receive one Tactics Card. At tactician level 5, it is guaranteed to be an advanced Tactics Card.

The first time you trigger Combine each round, receive one Provocation Tactics Card.

Bring your own firepower, then modify your weapon into different forms.

The fawn can wear equipment and immediately equips a Frozen Storm. +10 levels.

Magical Fawn · Twin

Magical Fawn · Resonance

Double the fawn’s level.

Magical Fawn · Devotion

When the fawn dies, divide its levels evenly among your heroes as Temporary Levels.

Giant Plush

The fawn gains 30 levels. Receive a Fawn’s Protection item.

Fawn’s Protection

Unique. 30% of the wearer’s incoming damage is transferred to the fawn.

Inspired Stroke

Chang Xiao'e
